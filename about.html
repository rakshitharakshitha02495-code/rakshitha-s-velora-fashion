The Velora Ethos

Founded with a passion for architectural silhouettes and luxury fabrics, Velora creates refined wardrobe staples for the modern woman.

Each piece is designed with extreme attention to detail, sourcing sustainable silks, organic wools, and cashmere from ethical mills across Europe.

We believe fashion should transcend seasons. Our collections focus on timeless elegance, durability, and effortless modern sophistication.
